DRONE VIDEO TO PROVENANCE-TAGGED 3D

A solo project of mine, built around a DJI Mini 3 Pro recording and its flight log. On that flight SSAKS cut 1,916 frames to 193, all 193 were registered, and a splat trained within a 3.7 GB VRAM peak. It is an early-stage pipeline: the Gaussian stage is working and measured, while the mesh extraction stage is still design only.

DECISIONS

  • DECISION 01

    Provenance Per Primitive

    Every primitive carries one of three labels (Observed, Prior-Guided, AI-Inferred) so mixed regions stay honest; the provider engine checks on every query that map data is tagged prior-guided with zero observation confidence.

  • DECISION 02

    A 6 GB Budget, Held

    The target is a 6 GB laptop GPU. A resolution-stepping scheduler (320x180, 640x360, 960x540), a FP16 image cache and a capped densifier keep the measured training peak at 3,663 MB.

  • DECISION 03

    Fallback Over Fixed FPS

    If no stable cruise segment appears within 30 seconds, SSAKS drops to motion-adaptive sampling instead of a fixed frame rate, since fixed sampling adds redundant frames that threaten the VRAM cap (ADR 0003).

  • DECISION 04

    Provider Independence

    Phase one uses only Overture Maps, OpenStreetMap and Cesium Terrain, with a flat-datum fallback, so no legal-review dependency such as Google Maps sits on the critical path (ADR 0005).

  • DECISION 05

    Sweep Before Trusting

    RANSAC threshold and geodetic-prior weight were swept against held-out error rather than assumed; a 3 m RANSAC threshold gave 1.0 m median error against 4.9 m at 10 m, but kept 95 anchors instead of 167.
