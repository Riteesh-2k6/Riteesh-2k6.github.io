DECISIONS

  • DECISION 01

    Custom Clinical Loss

    Combined L1, MS-SSIM, and Sobel Edge Loss to strictly preserve edge details and structural integrity crucial for clinical diagnosis.

  • DECISION 02

    Synthetic Degradation

    Realistically simulates low-quality X-ray artifacts (blur, noise, compression) rather than relying purely on bicubic downsampling.

  • DECISION 03

    Ablation Studies

    Conducted comprehensive validation across multiple loss functions to prove the superiority of the Clinical Loss approach.

  • DECISION 04

    Full-Stack Deployment

    Built a Vite-React frontend paired with a FastAPI backend for an interactive 'Before vs. After' clinical dashboard.
