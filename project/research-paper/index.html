DECISIONS

  • DECISION 01

    Interpretable Features

    Focused strictly on feature-based extraction (e.g., lexical diversity, burstiness) rather than black-box deep learning to ensure the detection process is fully explainable.

  • DECISION 02

    Syntactic Complexity Metrics

    Utilized advanced NLP parsing to quantify structural differences, proving that current AI models exhibit statistically significant structural homogeneity.

  • DECISION 03

    Stylometric Patterns

    Analyzed punctuation, n-gram frequencies, and transition words to isolate common generative artifacts.

  • DECISION 04

    Cross-Model Validation

    Tested the feature set against outputs from multiple different LLMs to ensure the detection methodology generalizes beyond a single model family.
