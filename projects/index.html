CHOWKIDAR

ACTIVE

CCTV THAT PHONES THE GUARD

A solo proof of concept I built for the Gnani AI challenge: the camera already knows someone is at the back wall at 2 a.m., so it phones the guard in Telugu, Hindi or any of ten Indian languages instead of sending an English app alert. What makes it robust is that plain rules decide every alert and read every command first. An LLM never raises or closes an alert, and an unclear reply never counts as acknowledged.

  2. 02RULESAlert Level From Site Config
  3. 03CHAINRing Roster Until Acknowledged
